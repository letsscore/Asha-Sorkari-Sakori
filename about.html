ABOUT US

Built for competitive-exam aspirants.

Asha Sorkari Sakori is a focused preparation platform for aspirants targeting government and competitive examinations.

Our purpose

We bring preparation, practice, revision and recruitment information into one focused competitive-exam platform.

What we offer

  • Free foundation learning with intentionally limited topic coverage.
  • Premium courses with broader topic coverage and additional important resources.
  • Timed mock tests and saved performance.
  • Exam-oriented current affairs and recent government job vacancies.

Account-based preparation

Registration is mandatory for both free and premium courses so your enrolments, payment verification and performance can remain linked to one aspirant profile.

Independent platform

Asha Sorkari Sakori is a separate competitive-exam platform and is not a school-education platform.
