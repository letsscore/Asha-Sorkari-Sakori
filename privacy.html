PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

How Asha Sorkari Sakori collects, uses and protects aspirant information.

Information we collect

When you create an aspirant account, we may store your name, email address, target examination, account timestamps, course enrolments, purchase requests, UTR/reference information and test results.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide account access, deliver free and premium courses, verify payments, maintain test records, communicate about support issues and improve the platform.

Payment information

We do not ask for your UPI PIN, banking password or OTP. For premium-course verification, we store the UTR/reference number you voluntarily submit along with the related purchase record.

Activity records

Registration, login, course enrolment, payment-verification submission, test submission and logout events may be recorded in Firebase so the platform owner can manage the service and resolve disputes.

Data sharing

We do not sell aspirant personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers required to operate the website, such as Firebase.

Security

Account and operational records are protected using Firebase Authentication and database security rules. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so keep your password confidential.

Your responsibility

Provide accurate information and do not submit false payment references. Contact us if you believe your account information is incorrect or has been misused.
